Child Safety Standards — Suitor

Effective date: October 17, 2026
App: Suitor — AI Dating Profile Optimizer
Developer: Prosper the Dev

Our commitment

Suitor has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) in any form, including child sexual abuse material (CSAM). Protecting minors is a foundational obligation of operating a dating application, and these standards describe how we meet it.

Adults only

Suitor is intended strictly for adults aged 18 and over. Users must confirm they are 18 or older to create an account. Accounts found to belong to minors are terminated, and any associated content is removed.

Prohibited content and conduct

  • Any sexual content involving minors, real or simulated, is strictly prohibited.
  • Grooming, solicitation, or sexual exploitation of minors is strictly prohibited.
  • Any attempt to use Suitor to contact minors for sexual purposes is strictly prohibited.

Violations result in immediate account termination and removal of offending content.

Reporting child safety concerns

Suitor allows users to report child safety concerns in-app. Every report related to child safety is treated as high priority and reviewed promptly by our team. You may also report concerns directly at any time:

Email: privacy@prosperthedev.com with the subject line "Child safety report — Suitor".

Enforcement

When a report or review identifies CSAE or CSAM:

  • The offending account is terminated immediately and permanently.
  • The offending content is removed without delay.
  • We preserve relevant evidence as required by law.

Cooperation with authorities

We report apparent child sexual abuse material and exploitation to the relevant regional and national authorities, including the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) where applicable, and cooperate fully with law enforcement investigations. We comply with all applicable child safety laws in the regions where Suitor operates.

Contact

Our designated point of contact for child safety matters, including CSAM prevention practices and compliance, can be reached at privacy@prosperthedev.com.
