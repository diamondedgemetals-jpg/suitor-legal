Privacy Policy — Suitor

Effective date: October 17, 2026
App: Suitor — AI Dating Profile Optimizer
Developer: Prosper the Dev

What we collect

  • Photos you upload for profile analysis.
  • Profile information you enter (bio text, interests, dating goals, app preferences).
  • Account information if you sign in (e.g. email address via Google sign-in, or an anonymous account ID).
  • Purchase information processed by Google Play Billing and RevenueCat (we do not see or store your payment card details).
  • Basic diagnostics (app crashes, performance) via Firebase services.

How we use it

Your photos and profile text are sent to Google's Gemini AI to generate your profile analysis, photo feedback, and bio suggestions. Account and purchase data are used to manage your subscription and free-tier limits. Diagnostics are used to fix bugs and improve reliability. We do not sell your personal data.

Photo handling

Photos you upload are transmitted securely for AI analysis and held temporarily to deliver your results. Uploaded photos are automatically deleted within 24 hours. We do not use your photos for advertising, facial recognition databases, or any purpose other than your profile analysis.

Third-party services

  • Google Firebase (authentication, database, storage, diagnostics)
  • Google Gemini API (AI analysis)
  • RevenueCat and Google Play Billing (subscriptions)

These providers process data under their own privacy policies.

Data retention

Photos: auto-deleted within 24 hours. Account and analysis history: kept while your account is active. You may request deletion at any time — see Data Deletion.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time via the data deletion page or by emailing prosperthedev007@gmail.com. We respond within 30 days.

Children

Suitor is intended for adults aged 18 and over and is not directed at children.

Changes

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Material changes will be reflected here with a new effective date.
