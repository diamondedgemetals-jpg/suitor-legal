Request Data Deletion — Suitor

You can delete your Suitor data at any time. Here's how:

Option 1 — In the app (fastest)

Open Suitor → Settings → Delete my data. This removes your account, analysis history, and any stored photos immediately.

Option 2 — Email request

Email prosperthedev007@gmail.com from the address associated with your account with the subject line "Data deletion request — Suitor". We will delete your account data within 30 days and confirm by reply.

Request deletion by email

What gets deleted

  • Your account and sign-in identifiers
  • Profile analyses, photo feedback, and bio history
  • Any temporarily stored uploaded photos

Note: purchase records processed by Google Play / RevenueCat are retained by those providers under their own policies; we cannot delete Google's billing records.

Automatic photo deletion

Uploaded photos are automatically deleted within 24 hours regardless — no request needed.
