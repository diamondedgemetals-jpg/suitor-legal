Privacy Policy — Suitor

Effective date: October 17, 2026
App: Suitor — AI Dating Profile Optimizer
Developer: Prosper the Dev

Scope

This Privacy Policy explains what information Suitor collects, how we use it, how we store it, and your rights with respect to your personal data.

What we collect

  • Photos you upload for profile analysis.
  • Profile information you enter (bio text, interests, dating goals, app preferences).
  • Account information if you sign in (for example, email address via Google sign-in or an anonymous account ID).
  • Purchase information processed by Google Play Billing and RevenueCat (we do not receive or store your payment card details).
  • Diagnostics and analytics (app crashes, performance data) via Firebase.

How we use your information

We use the information you provide to operate the Suitor service, deliver profile analyses and suggestions, communicate with you about your account, and provide customer support.

Photos, processing, and access

Photos you upload are transmitted over secure channels (HTTPS/TLS) and stored in Firebase to enable analysis, delivery of results, and limited support assistance. Uploaded photos are automatically deleted from active storage within 24 hours under normal operation.

Authorized personnel and automated support agents may access photos only when necessary to investigate or resolve support requests. Access is limited by role-based permissions, is logged, and is subject to internal review. We do not use uploaded photos to train machine learning models, improve models, or for any secondary purpose unless we obtain your explicit, separate consent in advance.

Third-party services

  • Google Firebase (authentication, database, storage, diagnostics)
  • Google Gemini API (AI analysis)
  • RevenueCat and Google Play Billing (subscriptions)

These providers process data under their own policies. We require reasonable contractual and technical safeguards to limit vendor use to the purposes described in this policy.

Data retention and backups

Photos: deleted from active storage within 24 hours. Account and analysis history: retained while your account is active or as required for legal or business purposes. When you request deletion, we remove your data from active systems; however, encrypted backups and logs may retain copies for a limited period (typically up to 90 days) for disaster recovery, legal compliance, or fraud prevention. While we take steps to remove data from backups as feasible, deletion from backups may not be immediate.

Deletion is irreversible

If you request deletion of your account and data, or delete your account through the app, the removal from our active systems is permanent and irreversible. After deletion, we cannot restore your account, profile analyses, photos, or any other personal data. Retained backup copies are removed as described above once they expire; these retained copies are not used to restore deleted accounts.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time via the Data Deletion page or by emailing prosperthedev007@gmail.com. We will verify requests to protect accounts and may require the email address associated with your account or other reasonable information to confirm your identity.

Children

Suitor is intended for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 18. Accounts identified as belonging to minors will be terminated and data removed in accordance with applicable law.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Material changes will be posted here with a new effective date.
